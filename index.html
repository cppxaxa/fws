What makhana is, and why we pack it

Makhana (fox nut) is popped from the seeds of Euryale ferox. FarmWagon sources it from Bihar wetlands, then packs it raw or roasted in olive oil — a traditional snack with a clean crunch.

Light, protein-forward crunch

A pantry snack with a satisfying bite, typically enjoyed as a lighter alternative to fried namkeen.

Sourced in Bihar

Handpicked makhana from wetland farms, packed for freshness rather than long, anonymous supply chains.

Two honest formats

Raw standing pouches for home roasting or cooking, and olive-oil roasted jars in Himalayan Pink Salt, Peri Peri, and Mint.

Everyday, not medicinal

We describe the food as packed. Nutrition, allergens, and serving guidance always follow the final label.

About us

FarmWagon is a registered trademark of Jagat Food and Beverages.

Jagat Food and Beverages is a Patna firm built around one Bihar crop: makhana. The work started with wetland fox nut — popped, sorted, and packed so a pantry snack from Mithila’s ponds could travel as a clean pouch or an olive-oil roasted jar.

FarmWagon is the name on that pack. Raw makhana for people who cook and roast at home. Flavoured jars — Himalayan Pink Salt, Peri Peri, Mint — for people who want the crunch already done. The kitchen follows standard food procedures and a hygienic routine while the FSSAI licence is completed.
